# Records and communication preparation

The free version is a database and a command library. It helps a service owner record incidents, track update promises, plan maintenance and prepare notices without building a new application.

Read-only HTML files give a dated view of the records. They are private local snapshots, not a live hosted status page, independent uptime monitor or delivery service. They do not refresh, work as a mobile editing screen, collect subscriptions, enforce viewer permissions or send email, SMS and webhook notifications.

Production status communication also needs independent hosting that survives an outage, tested publishing and delivery, subscription management and access controls. Enterprise DNA builds and operates those around the agreed service requirements. Keep the existing status service until the replacement has been tested. No command in this base sends or publishes anything.
